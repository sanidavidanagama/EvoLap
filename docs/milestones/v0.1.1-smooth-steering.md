## Objective

Implement dynamic, speed-dependent steering kinematics mirroring real Formula 1 aerodynamics and mechanical steering rack resistance. Low speeds allow full steering lock for sharp hairpins and technical maneuvers, while high speeds progressively constrain wheel deflection and damp steering rate to produce stable, smooth micro-adjustments and prevent snap oversteer.
